GEA (Kelvion) Plate Heat Exchanger and Marine Separator Models Guide

📅 Jul 29, 2026 ⏱ 16 min read

GEA/Kelvion — A Century of Heat Exchange Expertise

GEA (now Kelvion) is one of the world's leading plate heat exchanger manufacturers, with a heat exchange technology history dating back to the 1920s. In 2015, GEA's heat exchanger business was officially renamed Kelvion, after Lord Kelvin, the father of thermodynamics. With 67 branches and 5,000 employees worldwide, Kelvion's products cover gasketed, brazed, and welded plate heat exchangers, widely used in marine, oil and gas, chemical, power, food and beverage, refrigeration, and sugar industries.

In the marine sector, GEA/Kelvion plate heat exchangers and Westfalia oil separators are essential components of shipboard equipment, appearing on the machinery lists of major shipowners worldwide.

1. Gasketed Plate Heat Exchangers

GEA/Kelvion's gasketed plate heat exchangers feature a modular design philosophy — a single frame accommodates different plate series, providing flexibility for changing operational requirements. The core product line encompasses multiple sub-series under the N-Series (New Technology), each optimized for specific applications.

1. NT Series — The All-Rounder

The NT series is Kelvion's most versatile "all-rounder" plate series, suitable for virtually all conventional heat exchange applications.

Features:

  • Widest range of plate materials (304, 316L, 904L, 254SMO, Titanium, C-276, etc.)
  • Largest selection of gasket materials (NBR, EPDM, FKM/Viton, CR/Neoprene)
  • Broadest range of plate sizes, from NT25 to NT350
  • Excellent value with short delivery times

Common Models:

NT25, NT50, NT50M, NT50X, NT80, NT80M, NT100, NT100M, NT150S, NT150L, NT250S, NT250, NT350, NT500

Typical Applications: Central coolers, freshwater coolers, lubricating oil coolers, HVAC systems

2. NX Series — High Efficiency

The NX series (New eXtra) features a narrow gap design, developed specifically for high-performance applications requiring minimal temperature approach (down to 1°C).

Features:

  • Suitable for heat transfer tasks with close temperature approach
  • High NTU values for maximum efficiency
  • Ideal for district heating and cooling systems

Common Models:

NX50, NX100, NX150, NX250, NX350

3. NP Series — Highest Performance

The NP series (New Performance) takes heat transfer to the next level, with test pressures up to 42 bar(g), making it the first choice for data center cooling and high-rise building cooling systems.

Features:

  • Test pressures up to 42 bar(g)
  • Suitable for extremely low temperature differences
  • High NTU value applications

4. NH Series — High Pressure

The NH series (New High pressure) is designed for high-pressure applications up to 36 bar(g), suitable for oil and gas and chemical industries.

Features:

  • Adapted herringbone design for high pressure resistance
  • Available in titanium or special alloys (254SMO, C-276)
  • Pressure rating up to 36 bar(g)

5. ND Series — Double Wall

The ND series (New Double-wall) features a double-wall structure where two thin plates are welded at the port, creating a leakage detection gap.

Features:

  • No media intermixing for maximum safety
  • Plate rupture indicated by leakage warning
  • Compliant with drinking water regulations

6. NL Series — Large Gap

The NL series (New Large gap) offers plate gaps above 4mm for gentle treatment of high-viscosity or shear-sensitive media.

Features:

  • Reduced blockage for longer service life
  • Ideal for dairy products, food and beverages
  • Pressure up to 25 bar(g)

7. NW Series — Wide Gap

The NW series (New Wide gap) offers plate gaps up to 10mm, designed for media containing solids and particles.

Features:

  • Blockage-free operation with solid-containing media
  • Turbulence promotes self-cleaning effect
  • Minimal pressure loss with viscous media

8. NF Series — Free Flow

The NF series (New Free flow) features a special washboard corrugation with constant-width channels up to 12mm.

Features:

  • Constant channel width without contact points
  • Ideal for fibrous and solid-containing media
  • Alternative to shell-and-tube and spiral heat exchangers

2. Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers

GEA/Kelvion's brazed series offers the widest variety in size, brazing material, and connection types.

VT Series — Copper-Brazed PHE

The VT series uses copper brazing, suitable for standard water/water heat exchange and HVAC systems.

Common Models:

VT10, VT20, VT40, VT80, VT150

Features:

  • Compact design with no gasket maintenance
  • Long service life
  • Highest efficiency

3. Welded Plate Heat Exchangers

FA Series — Fully Welded PHE

The FA series features a fully welded construction for high-temperature, high-pressure, and corrosive media applications.

Common Models:

FA184, FA192

Features:

  • No gaskets, suitable for extreme temperatures and pressures
  • Ideal for petrochemical, oil and gas applications
  • Withstands high-temperature media

4. Westfalia Marine Oil Separators

GEA Westfalia Separator is a leading brand in marine oil separators, widely used in shipboard fuel oil purification and lubricating oil purification systems.

OSD Series — Self-Cleaning Separators

The OSD series is Westfalia's classic self-cleaning marine separator, featuring automatic discharge without stopping.

Common Models:

OSD2, OSD6-91-067, OSD18, OSD36, OSD55

Technical Data (OSD 6-91-067 example):

  • Bowl type: Self-cleaning
  • Application: Mineral oil (fuel oil, lube oil)
  • Throughput: Variable based on viscosity
  • Separation principle: Centrifugal

Features:

  • Self-cleaning bowl, no shutdown for discharge
  • Optional water washing function
  • Mitsubishi SJ30G gear pump for oil feed
  • Marine classification certified (DNV-GL, etc.)

OSC Series — Compact Separators

The OSC series is suitable for smaller vessels' fuel and lube oil purification.

Common Models:

OSC5, OSC15, OSC30

Features:

  • Compact design, minimal space
  • Suitable for small vessel engine rooms
  • Easy operation and maintenance

5. Marine Application Scenarios

Plate Heat Exchanger Applications

GEA plate heat exchangers are primarily used in the following shipboard systems:

  1. Central Coolers: NT250S/NT350 large plate heat exchangers for central cooling
  2. Freshwater Coolers: NT100/NT150 series for generator freshwater cooling
  3. Lube Oil Coolers: NT50/NT80 series for main and auxiliary engine lube oil
  4. Fuel Oil Heaters: Utilizing waste heat to heat heavy fuel oil
  5. Freshwater Generator Condensers: Brazed VT series for fresh water generator systems

Oil Separator Applications

Westfalia separators are primarily used for:

  1. Fuel Oil Purification: Removing water and impurities from fuel oil (OSD series for large capacity)
  2. Lube Oil Purification: Separating particles and water from lube oil (OSC/OSD series)
  3. Fuel Oil Changeover: Processing mixed oil during fuel switching

6. Maintenance Guidelines

Plate Heat Exchanger Maintenance

  1. Regular Cleaning: Chemical cleaning every 3-6 months depending on water quality
  2. Gasket Replacement: Gasket life typically 3-5 years, check elasticity regularly
  3. Plate Inspection: Check for perforation and corrosion, especially at weld areas
  4. Pressure Drop Monitoring: Increasing pressure differential signals fouling

Oil Separator Maintenance

  1. Bowl Maintenance: Disassemble and clean bowl per running hours schedule
  2. Friction Clutch Inspection: Check friction plate wear
  3. Seal Replacement: O-rings and gaskets need periodic replacement
  4. Vibration Monitoring: Stop immediately if abnormal vibration occurs
