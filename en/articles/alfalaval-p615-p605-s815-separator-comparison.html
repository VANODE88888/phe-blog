Alfa Laval P615 vs P605 vs S815 Separators: Differences, Performance & Spare Parts Guide

📅 Aug 12, 2026 ⏱ 16 min read

Alfa Laval marine separators are the mainstream equipment for shipboard fuel and lube oil purification systems. Among the P-series and S-series, P605, P615, and S815 are the three most frequently asked-about models. Many engineers and procurement staff are unclear about their differences — P605 and P615 belong to the same P-series but differ in capacity, while S815 is from the entirely different S-series. This article provides a systematic analysis.

1. Core Difference Between P-Series and S-Series

Before comparing specific models, it is essential to understand the fundamental technological difference between the P-series and S-series:

Comparison P-Series (P605/P615/P617) S-Series (S805/S815/S817)
Separation Technology Purifier technology Alcap technology
Oil/Water Interphase Manual — via gravity disc replacement Automatic — water transducer monitoring
Max Oil Density 991 kg/m3 at 15C 1010 kg/m3 at 15C
Primary Application Lube oils, distillate fuels Residual fuels (HFO)
Water Transducer No Yes (in clean oil outlet)
Controller EPC 60 EPC 60
Module Size 1650x1200x1100 mm 1650x1200x1100 mm
Module Weight 610 kg (incl. feed pump, heater) 610 kg (incl. feed pump, heater)

Key takeaway: The P-series relies on manual gravity disc replacement to match different oil densities, making it suitable for lube oils with stable density. The S-series features a built-in water transducer for automatic adjustment, ideal for residual fuels with varying density.

2. P605 vs P615: Same Series, Different Capacity

P605 and P615 both belong to the P-series, using identical Purifier technology. The main difference is separation capacity.

2.1 P605 Technical Data

P605 is the smaller capacity model in the P-series, suitable for smaller vessels or auxiliary systems:

  • Applicable oils: Lube oils, distillate fuels, biofuels
  • Max oil density: 991 kg/m3 at 15C
  • Max viscosity: 700 cSt at 50C
  • Separation mode: Purifier or Clarifier
  • Controller: EPC 60
  • Module size: 1650 x 1200 x 1100 mm
  • Module weight: 610 kg
  • Main supply: 3-phase, 220-690V
  • Control voltage: 1-phase, 100/110/115/230V
  • Frequency: 50/60 Hz
  • Control air: 5-8 bar
  • Operating water pressure: 2-8 bar

2.2 P615 Technical Data

P615 is the medium capacity model in the P-series, with higher lube oil throughput than P605:

  • Applicable oils: Same as P605
  • Max oil density: 991 kg/m3 at 15C
  • Max viscosity: 700 cSt at 50C
  • Separation mode: Purifier or Clarifier
  • Controller: EPC 60
  • Module size: 1650 x 1200 x 1100 mm (same as P605)
  • Module weight: 610 kg (same as P605)
  • Electrical specs: Identical to P605

2.3 Capacity Comparison

P605, P615, and P617 share the same module dimensions and weight. The difference lies in the bowl internal disc stack design and flow capacity. Based on Alfa Laval's official capacity curves (for trunk piston engine lube oil):

  • P605: Lower capacity range, suitable for small vessel auxiliary systems
  • P615: Medium capacity, suitable for medium vessel main lube oil systems
  • P617: Higher capacity, suitable for large vessels or high-throughput applications

Selection tip: If your lube oil throughput falls between P605 and P615, choose P615 for margin. All three models have identical installation dimensions, so future upgrades require no pipework modifications.

3. S815: The Alcap Technology Residual Fuel Specialist

S815 belongs to the S-series, featuring Alcap technology — fundamentally different from the P-series.

3.1 Alcap Technology Principle

The core of Alcap technology is a water transducer installed in the clean oil outlet. The working process:

  1. Untreated oil is heated and continuously fed to the separator
  2. The disc stack inside the bowl separates oil, water, and solids by centrifugal force
  3. The water transducer continuously monitors water content in the clean oil outlet
  4. When the oil/water interphase moves into the disc stack zone, the EPC 60 controller automatically activates drainage or discharge
  5. No manual gravity disc replacement is needed to adapt to different fuel densities

Alcap advantage: Residual fuel (HFO) density can vary from 960 to 1010 kg/m3. Traditional Purifier technology requires gravity disc replacement for different fuel batches, while Alcap technology adapts automatically, significantly reducing manual intervention.

3.2 S815 Technical Data

  • Applicable oils: Residual fuels (HFO), distillate fuels, biofuels, lube oils
  • Max oil density: 1010 kg/m3 at 15C — 19 kg/m3 higher than P-series
  • Max viscosity: 700 cSt at 50C
  • Fuel standards: ISO 8217, including ULSFO, VLSFO, HSFO
  • Separation mode: Purifier or Clarifier
  • Controller: EPC 60
  • Module size: 1650 x 1200 x 1100 mm
  • Module weight: 610 kg
  • Electrical specs: Same as P-series

3.3 S815 Application Scenarios

S815 is primarily used for:

  • Main engine fuel purification: Processing HFO 380 cSt and other high-viscosity residual fuels
  • VLSFO compliant fuel treatment: Post-2020 sulfur cap, VLSFO (density 950-991 kg/m3) is mainstream; S815's Alcap technology adapts to density fluctuations automatically
  • Mixed fuel treatment: Fuel bunkered at different ports may have significantly different densities; S815 requires no shutdown for gravity disc changes

4.1 When to Choose P615?

  • Vessel lube oil system purification
  • Distillate fuel (MGO/MDO) treatment
  • Stable oil density not exceeding 991 kg/m3
  • Budget-conscious (P-series typically slightly lower cost)

4.2 When to Choose S815?

  • Main engine residual fuel (HFO) purification
  • VLSFO/ULSFO low-sulfur fuel treatment
  • Oil density may exceed 991 kg/m3
  • Frequent bunkering at different ports with varying fuel density
  • Desire for reduced manual intervention and higher automation

4.3 Can They Be Mixed?

P-series and S-series separators can be installed in the same module. Alfa Laval's Flex module concept supports mixed installations with both S-separators and P-separators in a single module, treating fuel and lube oil simultaneously. This is a popular choice for newbuild vessels.

5. Common Spare Parts Guide

Regular spare parts replacement is essential for separator operation. Alfa Laval provides standardized service kits for both P-series and S-series.

5.1 Intermediate Service Kit

Interval: Every 2000 operating hours or 3 months (whichever comes first)

Contents:

  • Bowl O-rings and seals
  • Inlet and outlet seals
  • Water piping seals

The intermediate service primarily replaces aging seals to prevent leakage and maintain separation efficiency.

5.2 Major Service Kit

Interval: Every 8000 operating hours or 12 months (whichever comes first)

Contents:

  • Drive system components
  • Drive belt
  • Bearings
  • Friction pads
  • All items from the Intermediate Service Kit

5.3 Gravity Disc — P-Series Only

The gravity disc is the core component of P-series separators, establishing the correct oil/water interphase position in the bowl. Different oil densities require different gravity disc bore sizes:

  • Low density oil (e.g., MGO, ~860 kg/m3): Large bore gravity disc
  • Medium density oil (e.g., VLSFO, ~950 kg/m3): Medium bore gravity disc
  • High density oil (e.g., HFO, ~991 kg/m3): Small bore gravity disc

Incorrect gravity disc selection causes: bore too large -> oil escapes with water (oil loss); bore too small -> water enters clean oil side (incomplete purification).

S815 does not need a gravity disc — Alcap technology auto-adjusts via the water transducer, a major advantage of the S-series.

5.4 Bowl Disc Stack

The disc stack is the core separation element, consisting of dozens of stainless steel discs stacked together. The gap between discs is only 0.5-1mm, where oil is separated by centrifugal force.

  • Material: Typically 316L stainless steel
  • Replacement criteria: When disc surfaces show wear, corrosion, or deformation
  • Maintenance: Clean and inspect the disc stack during each major service

6. Maintenance Essentials

6.1 Daily Checks

  • Check separator vibration
  • Monitor clean oil outlet pressure and temperature
  • Verify discharge interval is normal
  • Check EPC 60 controller for alarms

6.2 Scheduled Maintenance

Maintenance Item Interval Content
Intermediate Service 2000h/3 months Replace seals, inspect piping
Major Service 8000h/12 months Replace belt, bearings, friction pads, full seal kit
Disc Stack Cleaning Each intermediate service Remove and clean disc stack

Conclusion

Although Alfa Laval P605, P615, and S815 share similar external dimensions, their technology and applications are fundamentally different. P605 and P615 use Purifier technology with manual gravity disc adjustment, suitable for lube oils and distillate fuels with stable density not exceeding 991 kg/m3. S815 uses Alcap technology with a built-in water transducer for automatic adjustment, suitable for residual fuels with varying density up to 1010 kg/m3.

The key to selection: know your oil type and density range. Choose P-series for lube oils, S-series for residual fuels, and they can be mixed in Flex modules. For spare parts, the Intermediate Service Kit (2000h) and Major Service Kit (8000h) are essential consumables, with P-series also requiring gravity discs matched to oil density.
