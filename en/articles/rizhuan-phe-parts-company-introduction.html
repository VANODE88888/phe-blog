About Taizhou Richuan Fluid Control Technology Co., Ltd.

Taizhou Richuan Fluid Control Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional enterprise dedicated to the supply of plate heat exchanger parts and technical services, headquartered in Taizhou, Jiangsu Province, China. The company focuses on maintaining inventory of plate heat exchanger plates, sealing gaskets, maintenance accessories, and separator spare parts, serving equipment users and maintenance organizations in marine, chemical, power, food, HVAC, pharmaceutical, and other industries.

The company adheres to the business philosophy of Quality First, Complete Models, Rapid Response, providing multi-brand, multi-model plate heat exchanger parts solutions to domestic and international customers.
