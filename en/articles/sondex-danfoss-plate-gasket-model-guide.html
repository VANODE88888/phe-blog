Sondex and Danfoss Plate Heat Exchangers: Plates, Gaskets and Old vs New Model Guide

📅 Nov 6, 2026 ⏱ 13 min min read

Sondex and Danfoss are effectively one family in the plate heat exchanger business: Danfoss acquired full ownership of Sondex in July 2016, and in January 2018 Sondex became a Danfoss product brand. That is why you will see nameplates reading "Sondex S43" on older units and "Danfoss SONDEX S43" on newer ones — the same machine, the same plates and gaskets.

This article clarifies both model systems, the plate specifications, and the "old vs new model" question that causes most ordering mistakes.

1. First, the Relationship Between the Brands

Date Event Effect on spares
July 2016 Danfoss acquires full ownership of Sondex Models unchanged; sales move under Danfoss
January 2018 Sondex becomes the SONDEX® brand of Danfoss Nameplate adds Danfoss; suffixes unchanged
Today Sondex lines remain in production Plates, gaskets, parts are fully interchangeable

Key point: an old nameplate reading "Sondex" and a new one reading "Danfoss SONDEX" with the same model number are the same machine, and plates/gaskets swap directly. What actually matters is the letter suffix (A/B) and the gasket version (Glue/Clip) — not the brand name.

2. The Sondex Model System

Sondex covers five structural families, more complete than most brands:

1. S Series — Gasketed (the core range)

The main product line, with over a hundred models from S1 to S380, covering 0.04~2.01 m2 of single-plate area. Full list in Section 3; old-vs-new mapping in Section 4.

2. SF Series — Free Flow (wide channel, anti-clogging)

SF11A, SF25A, SF53, SF66, SF101, SF123, SF131, SF150, SF160, SF229, SF230

Contact-free wide-channel design for fibrous, particulate, and viscous media (paper, sugar, wastewater, slurries).

3. SFD Series — Freshwater Distillers / Special Duty

SFD3, SFD5, SFD6, SFD7, SFD13, SFD22, SFD23, SFD35, SFD45, SFD222, SFD245, SFD307/10

Marine freshwater generator (distiller) and special thermal duties — one of the most common Sondex plate types in the marine industry.

4. SW Series — Semi-Welded

SW19A, SW19B, SW26, SW26A, SW40A, SW54, SW59, SW102, SW122, SW189, SW202

Laser-welded refrigerant channels with a gasketed second side, used in ammonia systems and heat pumps for both pressure resistance and safety.

5. SPS Series — Plate-and-Shell

SPS22, SPS72, SPS179, SPS400, SPS646, SPS647, SPS648, SPS1200, SPS1201, SPS1203

Fully welded plate-and-shell construction for high pressure and temperature (25 bar plate side / 40 bar shell side): power plants, chemical gas-liquid duties.

6. Brazed Lines: XB, SL, SLS

  • XB: XB05, XB06, XB12, XB25, XB37, XB52, XB59, XB61, XB66, XB71, XBDW22
  • SL: SL14, SL32, SL34, SL70, SL78, SL140, SL222, SL333
  • SLS (semi-welded): SLS14, SLS32, SLS34, SLS70, SLS78, SLS140

4. Old vs New Models — How to Tell Them Apart

This is where spare parts ordering most often goes wrong. The letter suffixes are not random:

1. A/B Suffix = Plate Revision

Same number, different letter means a different plate version. Typical mappings:

Old New Notes

S7 S7A 2.6 mm groove
S8 S8A —
S14 S14A 2.6 mm groove
S16 S16B Jumped straight to B
S19 S19A 2.3 mm groove
S20 S20A 2.6 mm groove
S21 S21A —
S22 S22A —
S41 S41A —
S42 S42A —
S43 S43A —
S47 S47A / S47B Two revisions
S62 S62B —
S86 S86B —
S100 S100A / S100B Two revisions
S113 S113G G = glued gasket version

Important: gaskets for version A and version B are not interchangeable. An S16 gasket will not fit S16B; an S47 gasket will not fit S47A or S47B. Always check whether the nameplate carries a letter.

2. Glue / Clip = Gasket Mounting Type

The same model often exists in two versions:

Version Meaning Gasket
Unmarked (standard) Glued Bonded into the plate groove with adhesive
Glue (S43-Glue, S65-Glue, S100-Glue, S130-Glue) Explicitly glued Requires adhesive fitting
Clip (S21 Clip, S22 Clip, S35 Clip, S58 Clip) Clip-on Snap-fit into the plate, fast replacement

Clip-on gaskets cannot be used on glued plates and vice versa — this is the root cause of many "leaks right after regasketing" cases.

3. "SE" and Other Special Suffixes

Some models carry SE (S21SE, S45SE, S55SE), usually indicating a special pressure class or connection configuration. Always quote the complete suffix.

6. Danfoss's Own Model Families

Beyond the inherited Sondex S series, Danfoss has three main plate lines:

1. XB Series — Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers

XB05, XB06, XB12, XB25, XB37, XB52, XB59, XB61, XB66, XB71, XBDW22 (double-wall)

Copper-brazed, gasket-free, maintenance-free; used in refrigeration, heat pumps, district heating, and domestic hot water. XBDW is the double-wall version for contamination-sensitive duties.

2. SL / SLS Series — Large Brazed and Semi-Welded

  • SL (fully brazed): SL14, SL32, SL34, SL70, SL78, SL140, SL222, SL333
  • SLS (semi-welded): SLS14, SLS32, SLS34, SLS70, SLS78, SLS140

Large brazed units such as SL70, SL140, and SL222 are extremely common in district heating and data-center cooling.

3. MPHE Micro Plate Heat Exchangers

Danfoss Micro Plate Heat Exchangers use thinner plates and denser channels for higher capacity per unit volume, mainly in heat pumps, refrigeration, and high-efficiency plant rooms.

7. Ordering Plates and Gaskets

  1. Quote the full suffix: S43, S43A, and S43-Glue are three different plates/gaskets. Quoting just "S43" invites the wrong part
  2. Check Clip vs Glue: clip-on and glued gaskets are not interchangeable — the single most common mistake
  3. Plate material: 304/316L standard; titanium for seawater; SMO 254 for high chloride; Hastelloy for aggressive media
  4. Gasket material: NBR (water, seawater, oil, -15120°C), EPDM (hot water, steam, acids/alkalis, -25170°C), EPDM-HT (high temperature), FKM (aggressive media, -5~180°C)
  5. Plate thickness: 0.5 mm standard; 0.6/0.7 mm versions exist for large low-pressure-drop plates — match the original
  6. Measure old units first: some pre-1990s Sondex models are discontinued — outline dimension + hole pitch + port diameter is the most reliable way to identify a replacement

8. Replacement Tips

  • Record the clamping dimension A before opening, and compress back to it after regasketing; large deviation means leaks or plate damage
  • Never clean plates with hydrochloric-type strong acids — 316L suffers stress corrosion cracking; use neutral or dedicated cleaners and soft brushes in one direction
  • For glued gaskets, apply adhesive thin and even, fit at half-tack, and let cure before assembly
  • For clip-on gaskets, press each clip fully home by hand; a clip not seated will leak in service
  • Inspect every plate against the light for pinholes; reject any plate with compression marks or cracks in the sealing area
  • Never mix old and new gaskets — replace as a complete set for even compression

9. Summary

Three points to remember about Sondex/Danfoss:

  1. One company — Danfoss acquired Sondex in 2016; models unchanged, parts interchangeable
  2. Letters are versions — A/B are plate revisions, Glue/Clip are gasket mounting types; gaskets are not interchangeable
  3. Quote everything — model + suffix + material + plate thickness
