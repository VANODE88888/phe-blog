Accessen Plate Heat Exchangers: Complete Series, Plate and Gasket Guide

📅 Oct 28, 2026 ⏱ 12 min min read

Accessen (Shanghai Accessen Ensign Technology Co., Ltd.) is one of the leading Chinese plate heat exchanger brands, serving heating, refrigeration, data-center liquid cooling, petrochemical, marine, power and metallurgy applications. Its gasketed plate heat exchangers are known for a complete series and wide plate portfolio. This article covers Accessen plate design, gasket materials, and all common models — a practical reference for selection and spare parts ordering.

1. Accessen Plate Design Basics

Accessen gasketed PHEs use two basic chevron angles across their plate portfolio:

  • L plates (small angle, approx. 30°): lower pressure drop, suited to high log-mean temperature difference (LMTD), general approach temperatures, particle-laden media
  • H plates (large angle, approx. 60°): stronger turbulence and higher heat transfer coefficients, suited to small-difference, high-duty duties

L and H plates can be mixed within the same frame to optimize performance — a core design advantage, and something to confirm with the supplier during selection.

Common plate materials: 304 / 316L stainless steel, titanium (TA1/Grade 1), titanium-palladium, SMO 254, Hastelloy C-276, nickel — matching seawater, chlor-alkali and aggressive media.

Common gasket materials:

2. Five Gasketed Series with Full Model Lists

1. AU Series — General Industrial (deep-slot distributor)

Optimized distributor zone with DN200 deep-slot ports and rich combinations of small unequal cross-sections. Suited to high LMTD, moderate approach, and particle-laden media — Accessen's highest-volume industrial series.

Models: AU03, AU05, AU08, AU10L1, AU10L2, AU10M, AU15L1, AU15L2, AU15M, AU20, AU20M, AU25L1, AU25L2, AU35, AU45, AU50, AU58, AU70, AU98, AU150, AU190, AU350S

2. AN Series — Large Industrial Cooling

High-performance plates with L/H chevron options for large-scale industrial cooling with small temperature differences — the flagship large-plate series with big single-plate areas.

Models: AN5, AN10, AN10M, AN10L1, AN10L2, AN15L1, AN15L2, AN20, AN25L1, AN25L2, AN25L3, AN30L1, AN30L2, AN30L3, AN30L4, AN35L1, AN35L2, AN35L3, AN35L4, AN40L1, AN40L2, AN40L3, AN40L4, AN45L1, AN45L2, AN45L3, AN45L4, AN50L1

3. AS Series — Steam-to-Water

AS10 is a short-and-wide deep-slot plate for large LMTD and small approach, aimed at steam-water heating; AS is the general-purpose gasketed plate.

Models: AS6, AS10, AS20, AS25, AS30, AS40, AS60

4. AC Series — HVAC Clean Water

Tall-and-narrow shallow-slot design for small LMTD, large approach, clean media — mostly HVAC pure-water circuits.

Models: AC15, AC25, AC35, AC45, AC50, AC65, AC70, AC98, AC150, AC180, AC190, AC230

5. AP Series — DN200 Mid-Slot General Purpose

L/H chevron options for standard LMTD and approach; highly versatile for clean to lightly contaminated fluids.

Models: AP5, AP8, AP20, AP25, AP35, AP75, AP98, AP120, AP165, AP210

4. Ordering Plates and Gaskets

  1. Quote the full model including the L-suffix: L1/L2/L3/L4 indicate different plate widths (heat transfer areas) within the same frame, with different gasket profiles. Quote "AN30L2", not just "AN30"
  2. Plate material by media: 316L for clean/cooling water, titanium TA1 for seawater, SMO 254 or Ti-Pd for high chloride duties
  3. Gasket by temperature: NBR for water/oil below 120°C, EPDM for hot water and steam, HNBR for hot oils, FKM for aggressive media
  4. Glued vs. clip-on: newer Accessen plate types use clip-on gaskets for faster replacement — specify the plate generation when ordering
  5. Never mix old and new gaskets: replace in complete sets and tighten to the specified clamping dimension

5. Replacement Tips

  • Record the clamping dimension (A) before opening the unit — after regasketing, compress back to the original A dimension; deviation causes leaks or plate damage
  • Clean plates with soft brushes and proper cleaning agents — never use hydrochloric-type strong acids (stress corrosion risk); inspect plate by plate after cleaning
  • Clean gasket grooves thoroughly before gluing — old adhesive and grease ruin bond strength
  • Tighten diagonally and in stages, verifying equal A measurements at the corners
  • Pressure test before returning to service — confirm no leaks before reconnecting to the system

6. Conclusion

Accessen selection is straightforward: general industrial — AU; large cooling — AN; steam-water — AS; HVAC clean water — AC; standard DN200 — AP, with AF/AM/ASD and welded series for special duties. For spare parts, the key is quoting the model down to the L-suffix and confirming plate and gasket materials.
